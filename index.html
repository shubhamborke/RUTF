Ready-to-Use Therapeutic Food (RUTF)

1 SACHET = 500 kcal
Dose recommendation: 150–185 kcal/kg/day
per child for 4 to 8 weeks.

For the dietary management of children > 6 months with Severe Acute Malnutrition without medical complications.

Usage Instructions:

Do Not Add Water

Do Not Heat

Wash Hands & Knead

Squeeze & Eat

Feed Child

Supplement With Breastfeeding

Ingredients:

Blend of non-hydrogenated vegetable oil (contains one or more of the following: palm, soybean, canola), sugar, whey powder, nonfat milk powder, peanuts, soy flour, fully hydrogenated vegetable oil (palm), vitamin and mineral premix.

ALLERGENS: peanuts, milk, soy. May contain traces of gluten.

Exclusive Breastfeeding

Recommended for the first 6 months of life, and continued breastfeeding is recommended for up to two years or beyond.

Use Under Medical Supervision

Intended as the sole source of nutrition in conjunction with breastfeeding.
Not to be used for Nasogastric Tube (NG tube) administration.

Once opened, discard after 24 hours.

Best stored below 30°C, in a dry place, away from direct sunlight
Packaged in a protective atmosphere.
